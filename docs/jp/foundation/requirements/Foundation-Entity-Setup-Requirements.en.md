# JPYdf Foundation Setup Requirements

> **Purpose and scope**: This document sets out, for review by external counsel, the jurisdiction-neutral core requirements concerning the Foundation's purposes, permitted activities, prohibitions, asset boundaries, and authority boundaries. The Foundation's operating agreement must not expand the Foundation's authority beyond, or weaken the prohibitions set out in, this document. Jurisdiction-specific incorporation documents, filings, governance procedures, actual addresses and signing thresholds, KYC materials, and personal information are outside its scope.

---

## 1. Basic Policy

DeltaForesight is developing "JPY DeFi," an over-collateralized CDP protocol that operates on Ethereum mainnet. JPYdf is a yen-denominated stablecoin that is minted and burned by the smart contracts of the JPY DeFi protocol. Borrowing, repayment, the opening, adjustment, and closing of a Trove, deposits into and withdrawals from the Stability Pool, redemption, liquidation, and other user transactions are initiated by users or other protocol participants and executed by the smart contracts of the JPY DeFi protocol in accordance with the code and rules then in effect. They do not require approval by the Foundation.

The Foundation is a non-profit entity established to act as a long-term **steward of the JPY DeFi protocol and ecosystem and as an administrative layer that supports the protocol and its governance**. This role does not mean that the Foundation becomes the issuer of JPYdf, a counterparty or intermediary to user transactions, a custodian of user assets, a protocol operator, a decision-maker or executor of Protocol Governance, or the owner of the Protocol Treasury.

Company Governance, which makes the decisions concerning the Foundation's activities, and Protocol Governance are separate lines of authority.

DeltaForesight Inc. (DFKK) is one contributor that carries out software development, a non-exclusive front end, marketing, community support, and similar activities, and shall not be made the sole or de facto controller of the Foundation or the protocol.

## 2. Purposes and Permitted Activities of the Foundation

The purposes and permitted activities of the Foundation are limited to those set out below.

### 2.1 Stewardship of IP / domains / documentation

- Hold and manage the intellectual property, trademarks, domains, and documentation relating to the JPY DeFi protocol and ecosystem.
- Support the continuity of the ecosystem through IP licensing and documentation management; provided that this shall not, on that basis, be treated as owning, operating, or controlling the protocol or its implementation.

### 2.2 Agreements with contributors / service providers / grant recipients

- May enter into and administer agreements with contributors, service providers, and grant recipients, including DFKK.

### 2.3 Off-chain acts incidental to Protocol Governance decisions

- Where a matter approved under Protocol Governance requires an off-chain act such as contract execution, recordkeeping, confirmation, notice, or filing, may record, verify, and carry out that act.
- Such off-chain acts are subject to a Company Governance Approval and may be carried out only where both a valid decision/record of the underlying Protocol Governance and the necessary execution evidence are in place. A Company Governance Approval does not approve, re-approve, or redefine the underlying Protocol Governance decision.

### 2.4 Management of Foundation operating assets

- Manage the Foundation operating assets received for the Foundation's own non-profit activities.
- Keep the Foundation operating assets separate from the Protocol Treasury, user assets, and other protocol assets.

### 2.5 Liability backstop / indemnity wrapper

- May act as the entity that assumes, defends against, or indemnifies protocol-related claims.
- The funding source for responding to, defending against, or indemnifying claims is limited to Foundation operating assets / insurance; the Protocol Treasury, user assets, and other protocol assets shall not be applied.

### 2.6 Private corporate governance and records

- CDRs, Member deliberations and votes, and approvals of Foundation operating assets are conducted through an access-controlled private governance process, separate from public Protocol Governance.
- Public disclosure is limited to the minimum scope, such as the RMI / MIDAO registry, statutory contact information, and any required smart-contract identifiers / public blockchain information; private CDRs and contract terms are, as a rule, not made public.
- Private off-chain governance does not rely on the public-ledger recordkeeping exemption, and retains minutes, votes, accounting, and execution evidence in accordance with applicable law and internal operations. The implementation of repositories, exports, and backups is defined by operating procedures, not by the OA.

## 3. Prohibitions on the Foundation

The Foundation shall not do any of the following.

### 3.1 Shall not become the issuer of JPYdf or an obligor for maintaining its value

- Shall not become, and shall not represent itself as, the issuer of JPYdf.
- Shall not issue or mint JPYdf.
- Shall not undertake, guarantee, or bear any obligation concerning the redemption, repurchase, or value maintenance of JPYdf.

### 3.2 Shall not become a counterparty / intermediary to user transactions

- Shall not become a party to a bilateral transaction with a user in respect of borrowing, repayment, the opening, adjustment, or closing of a Trove, deposits into or withdrawals from the Stability Pool, redemption, liquidation, or any other user transaction.
- As the Foundation, shall not conduct, intermediate, accept, reject, approve, or settle any user transaction.

### 3.3 Shall not custody / own / control user assets

- Shall not custody, hold, own, transfer, or exercise discretionary control over, for or on behalf of a user, any user collateral, assets deposited into the Stability Pool, assets recorded in or attributable to a Trove, assets transferred in the course of a redemption / liquidation, or any other user assets.
- Shall not hold any private key, signing authority, withdrawal authority, or other disposition right with respect to such assets.

### 3.4 Shall not hold protocol keys / roles / authority

- Shall not hold any signing key or authority with respect to the protocol's smart contracts, governance process, Protocol Treasury, risk controls, emergency controls, bridge controls, parameter setting, asset allocation, or any similar protocol function.
- Shall not serve as a signer, role holder, administrator, operator, controller, or any equivalent position with respect to such functions.

### 3.5 Shall not decide, execute, or act in place of Protocol Governance

- Shall not, directly or through any arrangement that acts on behalf of the Foundation, decide, approve, change, direct, or execute on-chain any matter subject to Protocol Governance.
- Shall not act in place of Protocol Governance, operate or pause the protocol, or otherwise exercise substantive control over the protocol.

### 3.6 Shall not own, manage, or dispose of the Protocol Treasury

- Shall not own, hold, manage, control, or dispose of the Protocol Treasury as the Foundation's own property, a balance-sheet asset, or a freely disposable asset.

### 3.7 Shall not engage in private inurement / ownership distribution

- Shall not cause the Foundation's assets, income, profits, or residual assets to inure to the benefit of any private person, or distribute them as an ownership distribution.
- Grants that advance the Foundation's non-profit purposes, reasonable compensation for services actually rendered, and reimbursement of expenses incurred on behalf of the Foundation are not included in the foregoing prohibition.
- Upon dissolution, residual assets shall not be distributed to any private person, and shall be transferred to a non-profit entity consistent with the Foundation's non-profit purposes.

## 4. Asset Boundaries

| Asset category | Definition | Treatment by the Foundation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation operating assets | Assets received for the Foundation's own non-profit activities | May be managed by the Foundation and shall be kept separate from the Protocol Treasury, user assets, and other protocol assets |
| Protocol Treasury | Protocol revenue and other treasury assets held and managed by on-chain smart contracts under Protocol Governance | The Foundation shall not own, hold, manage, control, or dispose of them |
| User assets | Collateral, Stability Pool assets, Trove assets, redemption / liquidation flows, and other user assets | The Foundation shall not custody, own, transfer, or exercise discretionary control over them |
| Other protocol assets | Assets attributable to the protocol other than the Protocol Treasury / user assets | Shall not be made assets of the Foundation |
| IP / trademark / domain / documentation | Ecosystem assets that the Foundation may hold and manage as steward | May be held and managed, but shall not be conflated with ownership / operation / control of the protocol |

## 5. Roles and Authority Boundaries

| Subject | Role / actor | The Foundation's authority boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | Steward of the JPY DeFi protocol and ecosystem / an administrative layer supporting the protocol and its governance | May carry out the activities in §2, but shall not carry out the acts in §3 |
| JPY DeFi protocol / user transaction | Initiated by users or other protocol participants and executed by the smart contracts in accordance with the code and rules | Shall not become issuer, counterparty, intermediary, custodian, operator, or controller |
| Protocol Governance | The then-effective procedure that sits outside the Company | Shall not decide, approve, or direct matters within its scope, nor execute them on-chain |
| DFKK | Candidate contributor / service provider / grant recipient | Shall not be made the sole or de facto controller of the Foundation or the protocol |
