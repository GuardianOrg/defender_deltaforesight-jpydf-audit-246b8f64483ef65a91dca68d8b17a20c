## 1. Scope

This Manifest records the expected addresses of the contracts and Safes that
hold Protocol roles on Ethereum mainnet Day 1. [`Governance.md`](../../../Governance.md)
is authoritative for what each authority means and which functions it covers,
[`Mainnet-Role-Holder-Separation-Policy.md`](../gates/Mainnet-Role-Holder-Separation-Policy.md)
for the separation conditions between holders, and
[`Mainnet-Safe-Role-Assignment-Gate.md`](../gates/Mainnet-Safe-Role-Assignment-Gate.md)
for Safe composition and readback conditions.

DeltaForesight's own-funds / launch-operation holders and the Foundation
operating-asset holder are out of scope here and are managed in their own
Manifests.

`address-fixed` indicates only that a checksummed address candidate has been
approved. It does not mean that Safe code, owners, threshold, modules, guard,
Timelock roles, or on-chain assignment have been verified. While any `<pending>`
remains, and until the Gate's `frozen` conditions are met, these are not used for
launch.

## 3. Reconciliation

At the final freeze, this table is not overwritten by hand to the current state.
The `holder-address-book.csv` generated by the Gate, the deploy / handover env,
`deployment-manifest.json`, and the on-chain readback are reconciled at the same
candidate commit and block, and that Evidence is retained. After mainnet
activation, on-chain state is authoritative for the current state.
