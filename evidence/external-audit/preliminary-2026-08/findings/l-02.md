## Reproduction and impact decision

During an unhealthy shared-JPY episode whose freeze window has expired, an
active feed constructor's initial fetch receives `freezeExpired=true`. The feed
attempts `BorrowerOperations.shutdownFromOracleFailure()`, but a newly created
source is not yet the branch's registered PriceFeed. The caller check reverts and
`JpyFreezeLib` catches it. The source therefore remains in `primary`, returns its
uninitialized `lastGoodPrice` of zero, and passed the old assertion that checked
only `priceSource`.

The source-level bug is reproduced and accepted. The report's claim that this
feed can then be registered and serve zero as a healthy production price is not
accepted for the current topology. `PriceFeedProxy` already reads
`source.lastGoodPrice()` in its constructor and rejects zero, then smoke-tests
both fetch paths; source replacement applies equivalent validity checks. A zero
source therefore cannot become the core's configured Proxy source through the
supported deployment or governance path.

The defect still weakens defense in depth, allows a misleading standalone
deployment artifact, and could become exploitable if a source were integrated
directly or the Proxy gates were bypassed. Low remediation is therefore
appropriate even though the stated liquidation impact is blocked today.

The pre-fix mutation run used the new regression without the constructor guard.
The Standard feed deployed successfully, causing the test's expected-revert
assertion to fail. The post-fix run rejects both active feed families.

## Remediation

`DFPriceFeedBase` and `DFWstETHPriceFeed` now define `InvalidInitialPrice` and
require both conditions after the initial fetch:

1. `priceSource == PriceSource.primary`; and
2. `lastGoodPrice != 0`.

This covers every active Day-1 source: dUSDC through `DFPriceFeedBase` and
dwstETH through its composite feed. A healthy primary fetch is unchanged. An
expired provider, collateral oracle failure, canonical-rate failure, or backing
unavailability cannot produce a deployed source with a zero initial cache.

Liveness is explicit: a fresh JPY round remains readable after M-01, clears the
provider episode, and allows the exact same feed deployment to succeed with a
nonzero price. The guard does not permanently prohibit recovery.

Archived candidate-only feed families were not edited. Standard archived
candidates inheriting `DFPriceFeedBase` receive the guard automatically if
reintroduced; separately archived composite implementations remain outside the
active scope and require a new activation review.
