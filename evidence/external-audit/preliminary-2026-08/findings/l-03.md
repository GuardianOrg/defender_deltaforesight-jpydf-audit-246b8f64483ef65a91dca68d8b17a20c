## Decision

The report correctly observes that partial Strategy receipts reduce
`conservativeValue` without changing `updatedAt`. It then treats the receipt as
an active maintenance signal and recommends setting `updatedAt` to the receipt
time. That recommendation is rejected.

`updatedAt` records the last successful full live valuation of both
`estimatedTotalUnderlying()` and `principal()`. A partial receipt proves only
that a known amount reached the Vault; it does not measure the current value of
the position left upstream. If the remaining position suffered an unobserved
loss while its valuation views were unavailable, refreshing the timestamp would
make the old high residual cache fresh again. Repeated small recoveries could
renew that cache indefinitely.

The current behavior is therefore intentionally fail-closed. A partial receipt
reduces double counting but does not extend confidence in the residual value.
After the original full-valuation TTL expires, pricing reports unavailable and
the oracle failure path protects the branch.

## Safety and liveness resolution

No production Solidity line is changed. The resolution makes the existing
boundary explicit in the JP/EN specification and adds a regression that proves:

1. exact partial recovery reduces `conservativeValue`;
2. the original `updatedAt` remains unchanged;
3. pricing becomes unavailable after the original TTL; and
4. once both live valuation reads recover, pricing availability returns
   immediately and permissionless `refreshStrategyValuation` repopulates the
   bounded cache; and
5. an incident writeoff reflects confirmed accounting loss but does not restore
   pricing availability while live valuation remains unreadable.

Confirmed permanent loss remains handled by the existing incident writeoff
path. Writeoff and availability recovery are separate boundaries: writeoff
finalizes the loss, while availability returns immediately when both live reads
recover; a permissionless full refresh then repopulates the bounded cache. This
preserves both safety (no partial-receipt TTL laundering) and liveness (live
valuation recovery needs no governance transaction).

## Mutation evidence

The report's exact recommendation, `cache.updatedAt = block.timestamp`, was
temporarily applied. The regression failed because the partial receipt advanced
the timestamp from `1` to `86400`, demonstrating the unsafe renewal. The
mutation was removed before the final test runs.
